Privacy Policy & Overview

Effective date: November 20, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Luna (“the App”, “we”, “us” or “our”) handles information when you use the application.

The App is built with a privacy-first approach. We do not collect, upload, store on our servers, sell, or share personal information about you. The information you choose to enter or create through the App is kept on your device, within the App's local storage, unless you independently choose to export, back up, or share it using a feature provided by your device or another app.

By using the App, you acknowledge this policy. If you do not agree with it, please stop using the App.

02

Information We Collect

We do not collect personal information or usage data. Specifically, the App is not designed to collect:

  • Your name, email address, phone number, postal address, or account credentials.
  • Your contacts, photos, messages, files, calendar, or other personal content, unless you choose to access or import something for an on-device feature.
  • Your precise or approximate location.
  • Device identifiers, advertising IDs, browsing activity, or cross-app activity.
  • Analytics, crash reports, diagnostics, behavioural events, or usage statistics.
  • Payment information. The App does not itself receive or store payment card details.

We do not use tracking technologies, advertising trackers, analytics SDKs, or profiling tools in the App.

✓No hidden profile: We do not build a profile about you or monitor how you use the App.

If a future version introduces a feature that changes these practices, this policy will be updated before or when that feature becomes available.

03

How Your Information Is Stored

Any information you enter, generate, or save while using the App is stored locally on your device. It is not sent to a server operated by us.

What “stored locally” means

  • App data is saved in the App's local storage area on your device.
  • The App can access that data to provide its features on your device.
  • We cannot remotely view, retrieve, or manage your locally stored information.
  • We do not maintain a central account or cloud database containing your App data.

Device backups and sync

Your device's operating system or your chosen backup service may back up or synchronise app data, depending on your device settings and the platform's behaviour. Such processing is controlled by your device provider and your settings, not by us. Please review your device's backup and cloud-sync settings if you do not want app data included in those services.

Exports and sharing

If you choose to export, copy, or share information from the App, that action is initiated by you. The destination app or service will handle the information under its own privacy policy. We do not control what happens after you share it.

04

Device Permissions

The App does not request access to device features or personal information unless a specific feature genuinely requires it. If a permission is requested, your operating system will ask you to grant or deny it.

Granting a permission allows the relevant feature to work on your device. It does not mean that the App sends the accessed information to us. You can review or revoke permissions at any time in your device settings, although some features may stop working as a result.

Where the App allows you to select a file, image, or other content, the selection is used for the feature you requested and is processed locally, unless the App clearly explains otherwise.

05

Sharing, Sale & Third Parties

Because we do not collect or hold your personal information, we do not sell, rent, trade, disclose, or share it with advertisers, data brokers, or other third parties.

The App does not include third-party advertising or analytics services. If this changes in a future release, this policy will be revised to identify the relevant services and explain what information they process.

External links and other apps

The App may allow you to open an external website or use another app on your device. Those services are independent of us. Once you leave the App, their own terms and privacy policies apply. We encourage you to review them before sharing information.

06

Data Security

Keeping information on your device reduces the need to transmit it to us, but no method of storing electronic information is completely risk-free. The security of locally stored information also depends on your device, operating system, device lock, and the security settings you choose.

We recommend that you:

  • Keep your device's operating system and the App updated.
  • Use a strong device passcode or other available screen lock.
  • Do not share your unlocked device with people you do not trust.
  • Review your backup, sync, and app-permission settings.

We do not have access to your device and therefore cannot guarantee the security of the device itself, its backups, or information you choose to share outside the App.

07

Data Retention & Deletion

Your information remains on your device for as long as it is stored by the App or until you delete it. We do not retain a separate server-side copy.

How to delete your information

  • Use any delete or clear-data controls available within the App.
  • Use your device's app settings to clear the App's stored data, where supported.
  • Uninstalling the App will generally remove its locally stored app data, subject to your operating system and backup settings.

Information already exported, shared with another app, or included in a device/cloud backup may remain outside the App. You may need to delete those copies separately through the relevant service or device settings.

08

Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect personal information from children or any other users. Since the App is designed not to collect personal information, it does not create user profiles or maintain a database of children's information.

Parents and guardians should still guide children's use of devices and review device permissions, purchases, backups, and any external services accessed from the App.

09

Your Choices & Privacy Rights

You remain in control of the information stored by the App on your device. You can choose what to enter, edit or delete information within the App, deny optional device permissions, and decide whether to export or share information.

Depending on where you live, privacy laws may provide rights such as access, correction, deletion, restriction, or objection. Because we do not collect or hold your personal information, we generally have no user data on our systems to retrieve, correct, or delete in response to such a request. You can manage locally stored information directly on your device using the controls described above.

10

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes to the App, applicable law, or our privacy practices. When we make changes, we will update the effective date at the top of this page and make the revised policy available with the App.

We encourage you to review this page periodically. Your continued use of the App after an updated policy becomes available means you acknowledge the revised policy, to the extent permitted by applicable law.

FAQ

Privacy, at a glance

Does the App collect any personal information?
No. The App is designed not to collect personal information, usage analytics, or device identifiers.
Is my information uploaded to a server?
No. Information you enter or create in the App is stored locally on your device and is not uploaded to a server operated by us.
Can the developer see my information?
No. We do not have a server-side copy or remote access to information stored locally in your App.
What happens if I uninstall the App?
Uninstalling generally removes the App's local data, but operating-system backups or synced copies may remain depending on your settings.
Does the App use ads or analytics?
No advertising or analytics services are included, under the privacy practices described in this policy.
